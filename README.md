---

BUILD THE COMPLETE "WORLD EDUCATION SYSTEM" — a world-class web app for FARFAZ.
Brand line: FARFAZ — One World — Education System For Ever.

PURPOSE
One system that unites the whole world's education. Every learner in every country learns their own country's real curriculum, taught by voice like a real teacher — step by step, in stories, with the text read along word by word — adapting to each learner so that nobody is left behind and nobody finishes a lesson without understanding it. It serves students, parents, teachers and school owners in one connected system, and it stays with each person for life.

SCOPE AND STRUCTURE
- All 196 countries. Each country teaches its own official curriculum and textbooks, by year and board.
- Path: Pre-KG / LKG / UKG, then Class 1 to Class 12, then colleges, degrees and career paths (medicine, engineering, IT, commerce, civil service, and every other field).
- Build order: India first — national CBSE/NCERT curriculum in English (covers the whole country at once), then the Tamil Nadu State Board in English, then every remaining Indian state board in regional waves in English, then each state's own language added as a layer on the same structure. Higher education and career paths are built alongside. Then expand country by country until all 196 are covered.
- Lessons come only from official published syllabi and chapter lists, and a teacher verifies every lesson before it is published.

TWO TRACKS
- Track A "School Book Track": follows the learner's actual school book faithfully — same chapters, same order, same topics — but taught in our own original words and stories. Never copy textbook text verbatim.
- Track B "Our Own World Track": our original education system, open to everyone — story lessons, life skills, money, culture, nature and livelihood (below).

HOW IT TEACHES (core experience)
- Voice-first, like a real class: every lesson is spoken aloud, step by step, with reading and writing taught explicitly.
- Cinematic story lessons: every lesson is a story told through rich animated scenes with a film feel — built as light in-page animation, never heavy video. The lesson text sits below and is read along word by word in karaoke style, each word highlighting in sync with the narration.
- Exactly TWO house voices for the entire system: Nisa (female) and Arshed (male). Soft, sweet, natural conversational delivery — like a kind teacher talking, never flat textbook reading — with correct pronunciation in every language. Each learner chooses one voice; the choice is remembered and carries every lesson, tour and narration.
- Adaptive teaching: small checks inside every lesson; if the learner is stuck, the system re-teaches that step differently, with more examples and more repetition, until it clicks. Young children get clearer, slower delivery with more repetition; older learners get a natural pace.
- Young children's poems and rhymes are animated song lessons with the text read along below.
- "How Things Work" interactive explorables: the learner taps a part of a machine, the human body, or a diagram, and the house voice explains that part while its text highlights.
- Move & Show activities (ages ~3–8): finger counting, thumbs up/down, head nod/shake, point-to-picture, tracing, clap-along action songs and sign-language starters, including for children who cannot speak. Camera use is optional and parent-approved, processing happens only on the device, nothing is ever recorded or uploaded, and a touch-answer is always available.
- Guided welcome tour: the voice greets the learner, asks their language and sets the whole site to it, then walks them through age, school and subject — for a child alone or with a parent, in every country.
- Student Tools: calculator, currency and unit converters, graph drawing, a pretend-money savings-goal tool, and daily study tools.

FOCUS RULE (never break this)
Inside a lesson, only that lesson exists — no unrelated material, no scrolling away. Inside a subject, only that subject, until the learner exits. Large, simple Back and Next controls.

ROLES
- Students: their own adaptive path, progress, streaks and skill passport.
- Parents: follow their child's progress; a voice-only mode for parents who do not read.
- Teachers: a free Core tier forever; a Pro tier (free while their school subscribes).
- School owners: one owner dashboard managing many schools in a single view, with the ability to feature schools; a world directory and network connecting schools and colleges.
- Employers (later waves): companies join in mutual benefit — posting internships and hiring trained learners, with step-by-step skill training matched to real needs. Never guarantee anyone a job.

AGE-ADAPTIVE DESIGN
The whole interface changes by age band. Ages 3–5: huge touch targets, big icons, voice-first, minimal text, very few choices. Then 6–8, 9–12, 13–17, and a full adult view. The learner's age is asked once in the welcome tour.

INCLUSION (non-negotiable)
The FULL system is completely free forever, with no barriers, for all learners with disabilities. Every lesson exists in multiple modes chosen in Settings and remembered per learner: Hear (full audio, spoken descriptions of every image and diagram, voice navigation), See (word-by-word captions and transcripts, visual-first lessons, sign-language video for key lessons), Read, and Easy (easy language, shorter steps, slower pace, more pictures and examples, frequent small checks and encouragement). Motor access: full keyboard and switch navigation, no time pressure anywhere.

LIFE BEYOND SCHOOL (lifelong journey)
- Careers: job and internship discovery and matching, step-by-step further skill training, entrepreneurship and business-startup guidance for each business type, and livelihood guidance including agriculture and farming.
- Money & Wealth track, staged by age: 3–5 recognising money, needs vs wants, first saving stories; 6–8 earning, saving, the three-jars method, goals, counting money; 9–12 banks, simple interest, budgeting, forms of wealth (cash, gold, assets) taught honestly — value can fall — and scam safety; 13–17 compound growth, investing concepts with simulated practice only, digital money safety, business money; adults: full investing, diversification, debt dangers, retirement. Principles only: never promise returns, no real-money investing for children, no get-rich-quick hype.
- Culture & Humanity track: each learner learns their own culture (a per-country, per-community path chosen by the family in Settings), learns about other cultures, and above all learns humanity — all humans are equal, from childhood. Teach about cultures factually and respectfully: no preaching, no ranking. Understanding between people is the purpose.
- Nature and animal care: lessons on raising and caring for cows, plants, trees, birds and the natural world — the system is useful for all humans, animals and nature.

CENTRAL AI CORE
An adaptive core learns new technology and curricula, detects each learner's misconceptions, warns early when someone is falling behind, and guides each person to exactly the education and skills they need — but it works strictly under the owner's control: the core only proposes, the owner approves, nothing ever self-publishes. Provide an owner panel with approvals, a full audit log and a pause switch.

CHILD SAFETY AND PRIVACY
Parental consent for children, data minimisation, no advertisements to children, no sale of data, full parent controls, and delete-my-data. Children's data is fully private.

BUSINESS MODEL (present this in the app; all prices are the owner's proposals)
Completely free for learners with disabilities, forever. Everyone else pays. Launch sequence: a free pilot for the first 50 schools, then a Founders offer (days 1–90), then Early (days 91–180, 25% off), then standard pricing. India: students ₹249/month or ₹2,490/year; Family plan ₹499/month for up to 3 learners; Career Plus ₹99/month. Other countries by income tier: $1.50/$15, $6/$60, $12/$120 per month/year. Schools pay per enrolled student per month (India ₹30/₹25/₹20 by school size; government and aided schools pay half), with group discounts up to 25% for owners with many schools. Teachers Core free forever; Teacher Pro ₹149/month in India, free while their school subscribes.

DESIGN — ULTRA LEVEL (this is the standard; do not deliver an ordinary template look)
Design this to the level of the world's most awarded websites of 2025–2026 (Awwwards Site of the Year standard), fused with the best-designed education platforms (MasterClass-level cinematic presentation; Duolingo-level purposeful gamification and character-led motivation):
- One bold art direction with a single clear point of view. Premium, clean, ordered and spacious, with one unmistakable visual hierarchy — the presentation standard of the biggest companies and institutions. Never cluttered.
- Kinetic typography: huge display type is the hero. Headlines move — stretching, sliding, reacting to scroll — using variable fonts. Serif display type for warmth against the sterile AI look.
- Motion narrative, not decoration: every animation must guide, confirm or tell the story. Cards and elements float gently with a soft physics / gravity feel and levitate at rest; sections rise and fade as they scroll into view; buttons answer every tap with a springy bounce, glow or tactile micro-interaction; numbers count up; page changes are smooth continuous view transitions, never hard cuts.
- Lightweight immersive depth: layered parallax, ambient glow, soft shader-like gradients, tasteful glass panels, one strong accent colour system, light and dark modes. Lesson scenes may use light interactive 3D-feel visuals (Spline-style), kept featherweight.
- The opening is a brand splash in the app: a cosmic nebula with twinkling stars; golden letters F-A-R-F-A-Z appearing one by one; "One World" in a colour gradient; "Education System For Ever" in an elegant script; two children reading together with sparkles. About 4 seconds, tap to skip, no watermark, light on basic phones.
- Best-practice education patterns: visual-first navigation (icons plus short words), card-based learning paths, progress maps, immediate feedback, streaks, a friendly character guide, separate child and parent experiences, per-user page customisation, bottom navigation for one-thumb use.
- PERFORMANCE IS LAW: all of the above must still run at a smooth 60fps on ordinary, basic phones — compositor-only animation (transform and opacity), small file sizes, lazy loading. Honour prefers-reduced-motion and provide a Calm motion setting. Inside a lesson, motion stays limited to the story cinema and the karaoke highlight, so focus is never broken. A beautiful design that lags is a failed design.

QUALITY AND HONESTY RULES
Mobile-first and responsive; tolerant of low bandwidth. A lesson counts as complete only when its animation, both house voices and its karaoke pass together — never ship a lesson with a missing voice. Label anything that is a sample or a proposal honestly; make no fake claims (no invented user counts, no guaranteed jobs, no promised investment returns). Interface launches in English with Tamil next, on an architecture ready for every language in the world.

DELIVERABLE
Build the real, working, ready-to-use application — not a mockup — screen by screen in waves: foundation and design system first, then India CBSE/NCERT (English) from Pre-KG upward, fully taught, then the roadmap above. At every stage, keep every feature in this prompt; drop nothing.
